# 0005 — Connected cluster contours

- Date recorded: 2026-10-20
- Status: Accepted in CoolQR 3.3.0
- Release revision: `06b29471d55f72081a2f25a0c781c8436fdf3bdb`

Rectangular bridges between modules produced knots in connected circles and
polygons. Shape the exposed corners of connected clusters and keep the shared
edges flush. This keeps connected silhouettes distinct while preserving the
light cells and dark module centers.

The previous bridge geometry is visible in the
[before sample](../../docs/images/rendering-review/before.png). See the
[contour specimen and catalog](../../docs/Verification.md#visual-evidence-and-limits)
and the [decoder and pixel checks](../../docs/Verification.md#verification).
